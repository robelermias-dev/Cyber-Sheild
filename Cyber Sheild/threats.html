What Are Cyber Threats?

Cyber threats are potential dangers that can affect computers, networks, accounts, and personal information. They can cause data loss, privacy problems, and unauthorized access to information.

Common Types of Cyber Threats

1. Malware

Malware is harmful software designed to disrupt computers, damage files, or access information without permission.

2. Phishing

Phishing is an online scam where attackers pretend to be trusted organizations or people to trick users into sharing private information.

3. Weak Passwords

Weak passwords are easy to guess and can make online accounts less secure.

4. Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security.

Warning Signs of Cyber Threats

  • Unexpected emails asking for personal information.
  • Messages containing suspicious links.
  • Requests for passwords or verification codes.
  • Unfamiliar email attachments.
  • Unexpected account security notifications.

Why Should We Learn About Cyber Threats?

Understanding cyber threats helps people make better decisions online. Recognizing suspicious activities can help protect personal information, devices, and online accounts.
