What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, programs, and personal information from digital attacks and unauthorized access.

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

We use the internet for communication, education, entertainment, and business. Protecting our information helps us avoid online threats and keep our accounts safe.

What Should We Protect?

  • Personal information
  • Email and social media accounts
  • Passwords
  • Financial information
  • Computer files and documents
