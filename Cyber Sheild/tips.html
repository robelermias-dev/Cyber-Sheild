Important Cybersecurity Tips

  1. Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts.
  2. Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.
  3. Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments.
  4. Keep your computer and applications updated.
  5. Never share your passwords with other people.
  6. Be careful when using public Wi-Fi networks.
  7. Back up important files regularly.

Cyber Threats and Their Protection

Common Cyber Threats and Prevention Methods
Cyber Threat Possible Risk Protection Method
Phishing Stolen personal information Check links and email senders carefully
Malware Damaged files and compromised devices Use trusted software and install updates
Weak Passwords Unauthorized account access Use strong and unique passwords
Social Engineering Exposure of confidential information Verify unexpected requests before responding
